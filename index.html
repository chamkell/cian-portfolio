The THROW App

My senior capstone project. Injury rates for baseball pitchers are at an all time high. High schoolers getting Tommy John surgery has become a regularity in the sport, so what's going to change to curb this trend? Here's how the project came together.

  1. 1

    Overview

    The THROW App is a Baseball Player Development app that uses velocity-based programming to ensure a healthy workload for pitchers.

    • Velocity-Based Programs
    • Individualized Player Plans
    • Education on Player Health
  2. 2

    Research and background

    The literature is out there to lay the groundwork for velocity-based throwing programs leading the way in health and player development.

    • Load Management
    • Injury Prevention
  3. 3

    Methodology

    Upon developing the app, a 9-Week program was built to optimally build me back up to 91 MPH for the first time in 3 years.

    • Self-Testing
    • 9-Week Program
  4. 4

    Findings and results

    Velocity-based throwing programs are an accurate way to manage fatigue and track daily workload in a thrower, properly preparing them for the demands of pitching.

    • THROW App
    • Health
  5. 5

    Conclusions

    Public education and research into accurate and accessible workload models will continue to push the needle in the direction of optimal health and performance for athletes.

    • Public Education
    • Workload Models
