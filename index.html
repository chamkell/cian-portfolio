The THROW App

Velocity-Based Programming for Long-Term Pitcher Health

Injury rates for baseball pitchers are at an all-time high. High school athletes undergoing ulnar collateral ligament (Tommy John) reconstruction has become far too routine. The THROW App introduces velocity-based metrics to safeguard athlete arm health.

01 / Overview

Targeted Workloads

Uses daily velocity feedback to dynamically auto-regulate daily throwing volume and intensity.

02 / Research

Load Management

Grounded in sports science literature to ensure athletes build capacity without crossing injury thresholds.

03 / Methodology

9-Week Protocol

Tested through a 9-week rebuild protocol that safely brought peak throwing velocity back to 91.0 MPH.

04 / Findings

Fatigue Tracking

VBT tracking serves as a reliable proxy for neuromuscular fatigue, preventing over-use injuries.
